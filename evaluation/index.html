Scope and interpretation

This exercise tests usability and evidence interpretation, not the correctness of participants or the completeness of the Moroccan AI ecosystem. An official target is not proof of achievement; an evidence gap is not evidence of absence. Completing an evaluation does not constitute independent institutional validation.
