Registry / Methodology

MAI-METHOD — How the registry works

Methodology, limits and changelog

What the registry documents, what it does not establish, how evidence is admitted, and how claims are corrected. The rules below are enforced by the validator and tests that run on every change.

Independent. TheMoroccoAI is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the Government of Morocco or any institution it lists. No institution has adopted or designated it as a reference.

01What the registry documents

Version 0.4.1, sources checked 19 October 2026: 41 evidence records, 65 entities and 78 typed relationships. Entity types: institution (19), company (5), initiative (3), event (1), project (14), legal instrument (1), model (3), dataset (1), technology (10), sector (8). Each record carries the source URL, a verbatim excerpt, the publication and retrieval dates, and one classification:

VERIFIED FACTAn event, act or status that the cited source documents directly (for example, a signature, a launch or an appointment).
OFFICIAL TARGETA quantified goal set by an official body. It is not an outcome and must not be read as achieved.
ANNOUNCED PLANA programme, institution or product an official body has announced or presented, whose operational delivery the registry has not verified.
INDEPENDENT ANALYSISAn inference drawn by TheMoroccoAI from two or more records. It is labelled as interpretation, not as fact.
UNVERIFIED CLAIMA statement attributed to a source whose underlying data the registry has not independently checked.

Sources by type: government portal: 14; organisation's own website: 6; government publication: 4; technical repository: 4; regulator: 3; state news agency: 3; peer-reviewed journal: 1; academic preprint: 1; independent list: 1; intergovernmental organisation: 1; investor publication: 1; secondary press: 1; signatory organisation: 1. Primary sources are preferred; secondary press may support only an announced plan or an unverified claim.

02What it does not establish

  • Completeness. The registry covers what it has documented so far. An absent organisation, project or sector is a gap in coverage, not evidence of absence in Morocco.
  • Use and outcomes. Public availability is not adoption. No usage, accuracy, performance or impact figure is recorded as fact.
  • Delivery of targets and plans. Official targets and announced plans are shown as such, never as outcomes.
  • Current operation. An operational stage rests on its latest evidence; operation after that date is not reconfirmed.
  • Legal identity. A matching name does not make two organisations the same legal entity; such links are labelled as associations.
  • Official status. Records quote public sources; they are not official statements, and the registry speaks for no institution.

03How evidence is admitted

  • A record is added only after the original source has been retrieved and the excerpt matched against it.
  • A lifecycle stage beyond "announced" needs a verified record that declares that stage. A signature, announcement, procurement notice or market clearance never establishes operation.
  • An operational or pilot claim states its basis (public availability, an operator's report of use, or an independent source) and names the operator separately from any developer or provider.
  • Inferences drawn by the registry are labelled independent analysis and need at least two records.
  • Candidates that do not meet these rules are not published.

04Unsupported, conflicting and outdated claims

  • Statements the registry could not check are kept only as unverified claims, with a note saying what was not checked.
  • Where sources conflict or are insufficient, the stage is "unresolved", with the reason stated.
  • A record whose quotation still matches its source but whose claim later evidence contradicts is marked superseded and can no longer set a stage. It stays visible.
  • Questions the evidence cannot yet answer are published in the evidence gap register, with what is established, what is not and what would resolve them. A gap must be re-assessed whenever a new record concerns it.
  • When the latest evidence for an operational claim is more than 12 months old, the questions page says current operation has not been reconfirmed. Elapsed time never changes a stage by itself.

05Corrections and reuse

Corrections are made in the data, re-validated and published as a new commit on the public repository, so every change is visible in its history. The record's verification note says what changed and why. Corrections with a source can be sent to agent@sohadot.com.

The data is published as evidence.json and ontology.json, with the gap register and derived files; Using the registry data explains how to read and verify them. No content or data licence has been declared yet; that is the owner's decision.

Each entry links to its commit on main or to the pull request that published it, where the merge commit is shown. Counts are those of data/evidence.json and data/ontology.json in that version; for registry versions the date is the registry's as-of date.   1. 19 October 2026 · pull request #6 · v0.4.1 · 41 records, 65 entities, 78 relationships

    Registry v0.4.1: published data documented and independently verifiable

    • A data reference page (/data/) describing the published files, identifiers, provenance, null and absent values, versions, coverage limits and which parts of Q8 and Q9 can be recomputed from the data.
    • A manifest (/data/manifest.json) listing each published JSON file with its SHA-256, so that a consumer can tell whether the files it fetched belong to the same build.

    • Evidence cards no longer extend past narrow screens when a quotation contains a long unbroken string, including while web fonts load or when they are blocked; the homepage navigation fits a 320-pixel screen.
    • No record, entity or relationship was added or changed.

    Corrections to previously published content

    • ontology.json carried version 0.3.0 and as-of date 18 October 2026 in v0.4.0, although its content had changed in that version; it now carries the registry version and date, and the validator requires both files to agree.
    • The registry index said the registry did not yet cover AI companies, models or datasets, which it has covered since v0.2.0.
