Registry / Methodology

MAI-METHOD — How the registry works

Methodology, limits and changelog

What the registry documents, what it does not establish, how evidence is admitted, and how claims are corrected. The rules below are enforced by the validator and tests that run on every change.

Independent. TheMoroccoAI is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the Government of Morocco or any institution it lists. No institution has adopted or designated it as a reference.

01What the registry documents

Version 0.8.0, sources checked 20 October 2026: 58 evidence records, 65 entities and 78 typed relationships. Entity types: institution (19), company (5), initiative (3), event (1), project (14), legal instrument (1), model (3), dataset (1), technology (10), sector (8). Each record carries the source URL, a verbatim excerpt, the publication and retrieval dates, and one classification:

VERIFIED FACTAn event, act or status that the cited source documents directly (for example, a signature, a launch or an appointment).
OFFICIAL TARGETA quantified goal set by an official body. It is not an outcome and must not be read as achieved.
ANNOUNCED PLANA programme, institution or product an official body has announced or presented, whose operational delivery the registry has not verified.
INDEPENDENT ANALYSISAn inference drawn by TheMoroccoAI from two or more records. It is labelled as interpretation, not as fact.
UNVERIFIED CLAIMA statement attributed to a source whose underlying data the registry has not independently checked.

Sources by type: government portal: 18; government publication: 14; organisation's own website: 6; technical repository: 4; regulator: 3; state news agency: 3; peer-reviewed journal: 2; academic preprint: 2; intergovernmental organisation: 2; independent list: 1; investor publication: 1; secondary press: 1; signatory organisation: 1. Primary sources are preferred; secondary press may support only an announced plan or an unverified claim.

02What it does not establish

  • Completeness. The registry covers what it has documented so far. An absent organisation, project or sector is a gap in coverage, not evidence of absence in Morocco.
  • Use and outcomes. Public availability is not adoption. No usage, accuracy, performance or impact figure is recorded as fact.
  • Delivery of targets and plans. Official targets and announced plans are shown as such, never as outcomes.
  • Current operation. An operational stage rests on its latest evidence; operation after that date is not reconfirmed.
  • Legal identity. A matching name does not make two organisations the same legal entity; such links are labelled as associations.
  • Official status. Records quote public sources; they are not official statements, and the registry speaks for no institution.

03How evidence is admitted

  • A record is added only after the original source has been retrieved and the excerpt matched against it.
  • A lifecycle stage beyond "announced" needs a verified record that declares that stage. A signature, announcement, procurement notice, market clearance or official gazette text never establishes operation: a gazette text shows legal establishment, not activity.
  • An operational or pilot claim states its basis (public availability, an operator's report of use, or an independent source) and names the operator separately from any developer or provider.
  • Inferences drawn by the registry are labelled independent analysis and need at least two records.
  • Candidates that do not meet these rules are not published.

04Unsupported, conflicting and outdated claims

  • Statements the registry could not check are kept only as unverified claims, with a note saying what was not checked.
  • Where sources conflict or are insufficient, the stage is "unresolved", with the reason stated.
  • A record whose quotation still matches its source but whose claim later evidence contradicts is marked superseded and can no longer set a stage. It stays visible.
  • Questions the evidence cannot yet answer are published in the evidence gap register, with what is established, what is not and what would resolve them. A gap must be re-assessed whenever a new record concerns it.
  • When the latest evidence for an operational claim is more than 12 months old, the questions page says current operation has not been reconfirmed. Elapsed time never changes a stage by itself.

05Corrections and reuse

Corrections are made in the data, re-validated and published as a new commit on the public repository, so every change is visible in its history. The record's verification note says what changed and why. Corrections with a source can be sent to agent@sohadot.com.

The data is published as evidence.json and ontology.json, with the gap register and derived files; Using the registry data explains how to read and verify them. No content or data licence has been declared yet; that is the owner's decision.

  2. 20 October 2026 · pull request #9 · v0.7.0 · 49 records, 65 entities, 78 relationships

    Registry v0.7.0: the online-services ranking corrected, JAZARI general assemblies and a per-institute operational-evidence gap

    • Two records, each read in full from its source: the ministry's release on the general assemblies of four JAZARI institutes held on 8 September 2026 (MAI-EV-0048), and the official Digital Morocco 2030 brochure (MAI-EV-0049).
    • The general assemblies are recorded as a governance event in the status of JAZARI ROOT, EduTech, Industrie X.0 and Smart City. No stage changed: a general assembly, and the ministry's description of a move to operational implementation, are not operation.
    • Evidence gap GAP-07 asks which JAZARI institutes have documented governance, staff, premises, operational activity or outputs. It is assessed per institute (ROOT, EduTech, Industrie X.0, Smart City, Nador) across eight evidence dimensions, with three published resolution paths that keep documented operation, documented non-commencement and documented discontinuation after operation apart; an institute that meets none has an unknown operational status. A legal approval, a governance event or missing records cannot resolve an institute, an institute that never operated is not recorded as discontinued, and an institute can be resolved while the others stay open. The validator and the example consumer check these rules.
    • GAP-06 re-assessed against the brochure; it remains open.
    • Not changed: the further ROOT signatories named in Bulletin Officiel 7534 are not added. The summary makes the State the party, represented by the undersigned officeholders (one of them the president of the CNDP, acting on the State's side); the signatory_of relationship has no published definition that distinguishes signing as the State's representative from signing as a party, so adding them would present each ministry and the CNDP as a contracting party in its own right.

    Corrections to previously published content

    • The Digital Morocco 2030 ranking objective (113th to 50th) was described as the e-government development index (EGDI), following the national news agency's dispatch, and compared with Morocco's overall EGDI ranks. The strategy's own brochure defines it as the United Nations Online Services Index (OSI), a component of the EGDI, with 113th as the 2022 position. MAI-EV-0002, MAI-EV-0046, GAP-06 and the Digital Morocco 2030 summary now keep the strategy's claimed OSI position, the United Nations' OSI values (which come without a rank) and the overall EGDI ranks apart. The 113th OSI place is the strategy's figure and has not been verified.
    • MAI-EV-0008 said the Bulletin Officiel text approving the JAZARI ROOT convention had not been located; it now points to MAI-EV-0047, which records it.
    • MAI-EV-0047 said that no act concerns the Al Jazari institute in Nador; the statement is now limited to Bulletin Officiel 7534 and does not imply that no other act exists.
