Acquisition

TheMoroccoAI.com and its reference assets are privately held and available for strategic acquisition. Confidential enquiries: agent@sohadot.com. This page separates what exists today, which can be inspected, from the owner's view of its potential, which is set out in the owner's thesis.

Independent. The asset is not a government platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the Government of Morocco or any institution it lists, and no institution has adopted or designated it.

01What could be transferred

  • The TheMoroccoAI.com domain name.
  • The site source: pages, static generator, validator, source checker and tests.
  • The evidence registry and ontology data: version 0.5.0, 41 records, 65 entities, 78 relationships.
  • The visual identity and logo.

No content or data licence has been declared. Bundled fonts remain under the SIL Open Font License.
