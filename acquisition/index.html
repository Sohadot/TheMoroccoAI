Acquisition

TheMoroccoAI.com and its reference assets are privately held and available for strategic acquisition. Confidential enquiries: agent@sohadot.com. This page separates what exists today, which can be inspected, from the owner's view of its potential, which is set out in the owner's thesis.

Independent. TheMoroccoAI.com is an independently developed digital asset owned and operated by Sohadot. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of any government, public institution, or third-party organization or company. References to external organizations, initiatives and sources do not imply their endorsement of or participation in the project.

01What could be transferred

  • The TheMoroccoAI.com domain name.
  • The site source: pages, static generator, validator, source checker, multilingual engine and tests.
  • The evidence registry and ontology: version 0.10.1, 63 evidence records, 65 entities, 78 relationships, with their classifications, identifiers and change history.
  • The visual identity: the hexagon mark, the site design and the social-sharing image.

These assets are owned and operated by Sohadot, and the definitive agreement sets out what is transferred and on what terms. Two elements travel with the site under their own terms: the source excerpts, which are quotations of their publishers' material, and the bundled fonts, which are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
