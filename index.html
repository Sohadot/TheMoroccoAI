01 — THESIS

Why this asset
is inevitable.

These six propositions are the owner’s strategic framing. They are not sourced findings. Sourced facts are in the registry.

MAI-T-001
Civilizational Positioning

Morocco has functioned as a knowledge bridge between Africa, Europe, and the Arab world for over a thousand years. The AI transition is not a rupture — it is a continuation of this structural role.

MAI-T-002
Category Scarcity

TheMoroccoAI.com names the category at the national level in one short, exact .com. A name like this cannot be recreated, only acquired or conceded.

MAI-T-003
Language Architecture

Four languages as infrastructure, not translation. Arabic, Amazigh (Tamazight), French, and English each unlock a different institutional audience and a different search surface. The English edition is live; the others are planned.

MAI-T-004
Governance as Moat

In an era of AI-generated content abundance, governed reference infrastructure with explicit source posture, versioning, and claim restraint is the scarcest digital asset class.

MAI-T-005
Strategic Timing

Morocco’s AI 2030 roadmap, the 2030 targets of Digital Morocco 2030, and Africa’s emerging AI market are converging. A reference layer has to exist before the market matures.

MAI-T-006
Buyer Logic

The asset fits organisations that need a neutral reference layer on Moroccan AI: global AI companies entering Africa, investors focused on MENA–Africa technology, research and policy institutions, and data or media businesses. No interest from any specific party is implied.

04 — GOVERNANCE

Source posture
and claim restraint.

MAI-GOV-001 — Editorial Doctrine

Every reference claim in the registry is tied to a verifiable institutional, governmental or academic source. The asset separates verified facts, official targets, announced plans, its own analysis and unverified claims, and it labels its strategic framing as framing. No claim is made beyond what the source evidence supports.

◆ Primary and institutional sources: government, regulators, intergovernmental bodies, signatories, state news agency
◆ Every registry record carries its source URL, publication date and retrieval date
◆ No phantom URLs — only live, verified reference pages published
◆ Versioned data with a public change history; no silent content modification
◆ Claim restraint: targets are never shown as outcomes, and framing is labelled as framing
◆ Multilingual roadmap: Arabic, French and Tamazight editions are planned, not yet published
