What has Morocco AI 2030 set as targets, and what is verified so far?

The Morocco AI 2030 roadmap, presented by the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform, sets 2030 targets of MAD 100 billion in added value to GDP, 50,000 direct and indirect jobs, and 200,000 trained and certified AI talents. These are targets. The source gives no progress figures towards them. The ministry's release on the state of the Morocco AI 2030 roadmap states its 2030 objectives of MAD 100 billion of value added to GDP, 50,000 direct and indirect jobs and 200,000 talents trained and certified, and, for human capital, the objective of 100,000 new digital talents trained each year by 2030. Release by the ministry, dated 8 September 2026. Its three 2030 objectives match the national portal's report (MAI-EV-0011). The 100,000 new digital talents a year is a different measure from the 200,000 talents trained and certified: a yearly flow of people trained, not a cumulative count of certified talents. The release gives no baseline, definitions or count reached for either. A target is not a measurement of progress. The registry shows targets as targets, never as outcomes, and records no target as achieved.

The roadmap is recorded as including 2 projects, on the basis of the ministry's presentation: Sovereign AI marketplace (digital platform, not an AI application; operational (public availability; use not documented)); Idarati AI (operational (public availability; use not documented)). Verified: it is implemented through JAZARI Institutes network. A project's status is not a measure of progress toward the quantified targets.

Evidence: entity page · MAI-EV-0011 · MAI-EV-0058 · MAI-EV-0013 · Q2 projects by stage

04 — METHOD

Method
and limits.

MAI-GOV-001 — Editorial rules

Every claim in the registry cites a source whose type is labelled, with a verbatim excerpt and dates. Primary sources are preferred; press may support only an announced plan or an unverified claim. Verified facts, official targets, announced plans, the registry’s own analysis and unverified claims are kept separate. The rules are enforced by a validator and tests on every change.

◆Targets and plans are never shown as outcomes
◆Public availability is never counted as use; no outcome is recorded as fact
◆Conflicting evidence is marked unresolved; outdated claims are marked superseded, not hidden
◆Versioned data with a public changelog; no silent changes
◆Coverage is partial: absence from the registry is not absence in Morocco
◆The registry establishes no official status, endorsement or legal identity

05 — THESIS

The owner’s
strategic thesis.

Framing, not findings. This section is the owner’s interpretation of why a structured reference on Moroccan AI matters and what role this site is intended to play. It is not sourced evidence, a government designation or a prediction. Sourced facts are in the registry.

“The intelligence of a civilization entering AI.”

— the owner’s founding line for the project
MAI-T-001 · Owner’s view
Civilizational positioning

In the owner’s view, Morocco has long served as a knowledge bridge between Africa, Europe and the Arab world, and the AI transition continues that role rather than breaking with it.

MAI-T-002 · Owner’s view
Category name

TheMoroccoAI.com names the category at the national level in one short .com. The owner considers such a name scarce: it cannot be recreated, only acquired.

MAI-T-003 · Ambition
Language architecture

Arabic, Tamazight, French and English editions, each serving a different institutional audience. Only the English edition is published; the others are planned.

MAI-T-004 · Owner’s view
Governance as a moat

As AI-generated content multiplies, the owner expects governed references, with explicit sources, versions and claim restraint, to become scarce and valuable.

MAI-T-005 · Owner’s view
Timing

Morocco’s AI 2030 roadmap and the 2030 targets of Digital Morocco 2030 are recorded in the registry as targets. The owner’s view is that a reference layer is most useful before the market matures.

MAI-T-006 · Intended role
Category-level reference

The intended role is a category-level reference on Moroccan AI. That role is an aim: no institution has adopted, endorsed or designated the site.

Framing vocabulary

The owner’s working terms. They are not part of the registry’s ontology, which is published as /data/ontology.json.

MAI-O-001
AI Readiness
The institutional, infrastructural, regulatory and talent capacity of a nation to absorb and produce AI systems at scale.
MAI-O-002
Civilizational Node
A geographic and cultural position that works as a translation and routing layer between knowledge traditions.
MAI-O-003
Sovereign Reference
A governed knowledge resource that defines category terms without depending on a single institutional authority. Used here as an aim, not as a claim of official status.
MAI-O-004
Language Architecture
Structuring multilingual content as institutional access layers, not as translations of one primary text.
MAI-O-005
Category Anchor
A domain whose name, structure and reference depth could make it the default reference for a category. A strategic aim.
MAI-O-006
Governance Debt
The accumulated risk of an AI ecosystem that grows without explicit sources, claim restraint or versioned accountability.
