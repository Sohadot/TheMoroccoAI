Q6Which AI applications are deployed, who operates them, and how current is the evidence?

Every AI application in the registry, with its operator (distinct from any provider or developer), sector, lifecycle stage, the basis of that stage, and the date of the latest status evidence. Deployment is not evidence of performance or outcomes, and none are recorded here.

Q9What does the registry establish about national targets, and which outcomes remain unverified?

Each official target as its source states it, the projects the strategy is recorded as including, and what the registry records about measured progress. Targets are never shown as outcomes, and a project's status is not progress toward a target. A strategy can have several target records: besides its own objectives, targets set in the responsible ministry's finance-law performance documents, some of which predate the strategy. Each row's source says which document set the target; where targets conflict, the conflict is recorded in the strategy's gap, not resolved.
