Digital Morocco 2030

Maroc Digital 2030 · Morocco Digital 2030

National digital strategy launched on 25 September 2024. Its official targets include 240,000 direct jobs and a rise from 113th to 50th place worldwide on the United Nations ranking of online public services, which the strategy's brochure defines as the Online Services Index, a component of the e-government development index; the 113th place is the strategy's figure, not verified by the registry. The finance ministry's note on the 2025 finance bill states the same objective as the top 50 on the overall e-government development index; the registry does not reconcile the two.
