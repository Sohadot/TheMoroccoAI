DeepEcho AI ultrasound software

Software that uses deep learning to assist ultrasound video diagnosis in prenatal care, including automated fetal measurements. Its developer states that its claims are not yet validated by the FDA and that its clinical studies are under scientific and regulatory review.

    DeepEcho website: product description and regulatory disclaimer

    DeepEcho describes its software as using AI and deep learning to help radiologists and minimally trained clinicians with ultrasound video diagnosis, and states on its website that its claims and metrics are not yet validated by the FDA and come from its own clinical studies under scientific and regulatory review.

    “Deepecho uses Artificial Intelligence and Deep Learning to mimic what a trained sonographer would do and help radiologists and minimally trained clinicians with ultrasound video diagnosis.”

    “Claims, figures, metrics & any kind of scientific and / or marketing materials listed in the current website are not validated yet by the FDA.”
