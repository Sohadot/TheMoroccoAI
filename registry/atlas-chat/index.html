02Evidence

  • MAI-EV-0021VERIFIED FACT

    Atlas-Chat: Adapting Large Language Models for Low-Resource Moroccan Arabic Dialect (arXiv:2409.17912)

    A preprint first submitted on 26 September 2024 introduces Atlas-Chat, large language models for Moroccan Arabic (Darija) in 2B, 9B and 27B sizes, fine-tuned on an instruction dataset built by consolidating existing Darija resources, creating new manual and synthetic data, and translating English instructions.

    “Atlas-Chat: Adapting Large Language Models for Low-Resource Moroccan Arabic Dialect”

    “Focusing on Moroccan Arabic, also known as Darija, we construct our instruction dataset by consolidating existing Darija language resources, creating novel datasets both manually and synthetically, and translating English instructions with stringent quality control.”

    “Atlas-Chat-2B, 9B, and 27B models, fine-tuned on the dataset”

    Darija-SFT-Mixture is published on Hugging Face as an instruction dataset of 458K samples in Darija, released under the ODC-BY licence with the note that some subsets carry non-commercial terms.

    Atlas-Chat-9B model card

    Atlas-Chat is published on Hugging Face as a family of open models instruction-tuned for Darija. The 9B model card names MBZUAI France Lab, a Paris research centre affiliated with MBZUAI in Abu Dhabi, as developer; lists the Gemma licence; and gives google/gemma-2-9b-it as the model it was fine-tuned from.
