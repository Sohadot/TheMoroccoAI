Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform

Ministère de la Transition Numérique et de la Réforme de l'Administration · MTNRA

The government department that launched Digital Morocco 2030 in September 2024, organised the First National AI Conference in July 2025 and presented the Morocco AI 2030 roadmap in September 2026.

Note. The government appointed on 16 October 2026 includes a Minister of Artificial Intelligence, Digital Transformation and Administration Reform. The registry has not yet verified whether that ministry takes over this department's portfolio.
