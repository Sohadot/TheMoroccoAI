Morocco AI 2030

AI Made in Morocco · Maroc IA 2030

National AI roadmap presented by the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform. It is built on three pillars and relies on the JAZARI Institutes network. Its official 2030 targets are MAD 100 billion in added value to GDP, 50,000 jobs and 200,000 certified AI talents.

    Maroc IA 2030: presentation of the roadmap's "main advances"

    The national portal reports that the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform presented in Rabat what it calls the main advances of the Maroc IA 2030 roadmap. The article describes the roadmap as resting on three pillars and ten structuring programmes, and lists the items presented: the sovereign AI marketplace, Idarati AI, a proof of concept of the national meta-application and e-wallet, and a "Livre Rose" on digital sovereignty.

    “a présenté, mardi à Rabat, les principales avancées de la feuille de route "Maroc IA 2030"”

    “Cette feuille de route repose sur trois grands socles”

    “outre dix programmes structurants”

    “Parmi les premières solutions figure Idarati AI”

    “un Proof of Concept (POC) de la méta-application et du e-wallet national”

  • MAI-EV-0013ANNOUNCED PLAN

    Sovereign AI marketplace and the Idarati AI assistant presented

    The ministry unveiled a sovereign AI marketplace intended to give access to secure and trusted AI solutions hosted under Moroccan control, and presented Idarati AI, an agentic AI assistant intended to help users with administrative procedures.

    “The ministry also unveiled a sovereign AI marketplace designed to provide access to secure and trusted AI solutions while ensuring data protection and maintaining control over infrastructure in Morocco.”

    “One of the first solutions presented was Idarati AI, an agentic AI assistant intended to inform, guide and assist users with administrative procedures.”

  • MAI-EV-0012VERIFIED FACT

    Morocco AI 2030 roadmap: pillars and the JAZARI Institutes network

    The Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform presented the Morocco AI 2030 roadmap in Rabat in September 2026. As presented, it is structured around three pillars (sovereignty and trust; innovation and competitiveness; impact, adoption and outreach) and relies on the national JAZARI Institutes network, described as comprising JAZARI ROOT, JAZARI Smart City, JAZARI EduTech and JAZARI Industrie X.0.

    “Morocco’s Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform on Tuesday in Rabat presented the main advances under the "Morocco AI 2030" roadmap”

    “The strategy is built around three pillars: sovereignty and trust, innovation and competitiveness, and impact, adoption and outreach.”

    “Its implementation notably relies on the national network of JAZARI Institutes, the operational arm for AI research and innovation, comprising JAZARI ROOT, JAZARI Smart City, JAZARI EduTech and JAZARI Industrie X.0.”

  • MAI-EV-0058OFFICIAL TARGET

    Ministry release: Morocco AI 2030 targets and the annual digital-talent objective

    The ministry's release on the state of the Morocco AI 2030 roadmap states its 2030 objectives of MAD 100 billion of value added to GDP, 50,000 direct and indirect jobs and 200,000 talents trained and certified, and, for human capital, the objective of 100,000 new digital talents trained each year by 2030.

    “À l’horizon 2030, la feuille de route fixe des objectifs consistant notamment à générer 100 milliards de dirhams de valeur ajoutée au produit intérieur brut, à créer 50 000 emplois directs et indirects, ainsi qu’à former et certifier 200 000 talents et compétences.”

    “En matière de capital humain, les différents programmes visant à élargir la base des compétences nationales ont été présentés, avec l’objectif d’atteindre 100 000 nouveaux talents numériques formés chaque année à l’horizon 2030.”

    Release by the ministry, dated 8 September 2026. Its three 2030 objectives match the national portal's report (MAI-EV-0011). The 100,000 new digital talents a year is a different measure from the 200,000 talents trained and certified: a yearly flow of people trained, not a cumulative count of certified talents. The release gives no baseline, definitions or count reached for either. A target is not a measurement of progress.
