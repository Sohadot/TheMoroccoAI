Morocco AI 2030

AI Made in Morocco · Maroc IA 2030

National AI roadmap presented by the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform. It is built on three pillars and relies on the JAZARI Institutes network. Its official 2030 targets are MAD 100 billion in added value to GDP, 50,000 jobs and 200,000 certified AI talents.

    Sovereign AI marketplace and the Idarati AI assistant presented

    The ministry unveiled a sovereign AI marketplace intended to give access to secure and trusted AI solutions hosted under Moroccan control, and presented Idarati AI, an agentic AI assistant intended to help users with administrative procedures.

    “The ministry also unveiled a sovereign AI marketplace designed to provide access to secure and trusted AI solutions while ensuring data protection and maintaining control over infrastructure in Morocco.”

    “One of the first solutions presented was Idarati AI, an agentic AI assistant intended to inform, guide and assist users with administrative procedures.”

  • MAI-EV-0012VERIFIED FACT

    Morocco AI 2030 roadmap: pillars and the JAZARI Institutes network

    The Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform presented the Morocco AI 2030 roadmap in Rabat in September 2026. As presented, it is structured around three pillars (sovereignty and trust; innovation and competitiveness; impact, adoption and outreach) and relies on the national JAZARI Institutes network, described as comprising JAZARI ROOT, JAZARI Smart City, JAZARI EduTech and JAZARI Industrie X.0.

    “Morocco’s Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform on Tuesday in Rabat presented the main advances under the "Morocco AI 2030" roadmap”

    “The strategy is built around three pillars: sovereignty and trust, innovation and competitiveness, and impact, adoption and outreach.”

    “Its implementation notably relies on the national network of JAZARI Institutes, the operational arm for AI research and innovation, comprising JAZARI ROOT, JAZARI Smart City, JAZARI EduTech and JAZARI Industrie X.0.”

  • MAI-EV-0011OFFICIAL TARGET

    Morocco AI 2030 roadmap: 2030 targets

    The Morocco AI 2030 roadmap, presented by the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform, sets 2030 targets of MAD 100 billion in added value to GDP, 50,000 direct and indirect jobs, and 200,000 trained and certified AI talents.

    “Morocco’s Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform on Tuesday in Rabat presented the main advances under the "Morocco AI 2030" roadmap”

    “The roadmap, also referred to as “AI Made in Morocco,” aims by 2030 to generate 100 billion dirhams ($10 billion) in added value for the country’s GDP, create 50,000 direct and indirect jobs, and train and certify 200,000 AI talents”
