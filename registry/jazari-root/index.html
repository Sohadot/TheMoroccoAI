JAZARI ROOT

CGEM and several ministerial departments signed its constitutive convention on 12 January 2026.

  • MAI-EV-0012VERIFIED FACT

    Morocco AI 2030 roadmap: pillars and the JAZARI Institutes network

    The Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform presented the Morocco AI 2030 roadmap in Rabat in September 2026. As presented, it is structured around three pillars (sovereignty and trust; innovation and competitiveness; impact, adoption and outreach) and relies on the national JAZARI Institutes network, described as comprising JAZARI ROOT, JAZARI Smart City, JAZARI EduTech and JAZARI Industrie X.0.

    “Morocco’s Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform on Tuesday in Rabat presented the main advances under the "Morocco AI 2030" roadmap”

    “The strategy is built around three pillars: sovereignty and trust, innovation and competitiveness, and impact, adoption and outreach.”

    “Its implementation notably relies on the national network of JAZARI Institutes, the operational arm for AI research and innovation, comprising JAZARI ROOT, JAZARI Smart City, JAZARI EduTech and JAZARI Industrie X.0.”

  • MAI-EV-0047VERIFIED FACT

    Bulletin Officiel 7534: joint orders approving the founding conventions of four JAZARI institutes

    Bulletin Officiel no. 7534 of 13 August 2026 publishes four joint orders of the Minister Delegate for Digital Transition and Administration Reform and the Minister Delegate for the Budget approving the founding conventions of four public-interest groups: JAZARI ROOT (order 1535.26 of 16 July 2026) and JAZARI Education and Technological Innovation (1618.26), JAZARI Industrie X.0 (1619.26) and JAZARI Smart City (1620.26), all of 22 July 2026, each followed by a published summary of the convention. The summaries list the president of CGEM among the founding members of JAZARI ROOT, and the Ministries of Economy and Finance, Industry and Commerce, and Digital Transition, with the Euro-Mediterranean University of Fès, Sidi Mohammed Ben Abdellah University, Moulay Ismail University and Al Akhawayn University, as the founding members of JAZARI Industrie X.0.

    “قرار مشترك للوزيرة المنتدبة لدى رئيس الحكومة المكلفة بالانتقال الرقمي وإصلاح الإدارة والوزير المنتدب لدى وزيرة الاقتصاد والمالية المكلف بالميزانية رقم 1535.26 صادر في فاتح صفر 1448 (16 يوليو 2026) يقضي بالمصادقة على الاتفاقية المؤسسة للمجموعة ذات النفع العام المسماة «معهد جزري النواة».”

Excerpts were transcribed from the page images, because the PDF's text layer reverses Arabic word order; automated excerpt matching cannot be relied on for this record. The orders approve conventions that are annexed to the original orders and not reproduced: only summaries are published, so this is a legal establishment act, not the signed text, and it gives no signing dates. Each group is created for thirty years with a statutory seat (JAZARI Industrie X.0 at the Euro-Mediterranean University of Fès). Legal establishment is not operation: the gazette does not show premises in use, staff or activity. The summaries name further founding members (for JAZARI ROOT, the Secretary-General of the Government, several ministries, national police, the CNDP, the Digital Development Agency and the Rabat-Salé-Kénitra regional council; for the other two groups, further ministries and public bodies) that are not recorded as relationships here. No act concerns the Al Jazari institute in Nador.

  • MAI-EV-0010ANNOUNCED PLAN

    JAZARI network announced across the twelve regions; JAZARI ROOT as its core

    In January 2026 the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform announced a network of "Jazari" institutes of excellence anchored in the twelve regions of the Kingdom, with JAZARI ROOT as its technical and strategic core, organised around e-government, smart rural and mountain zones, and major sporting events.

    “l’ annonce, en janvier dernier, par le ministère de la transition numérique et de la réforme de l’administration de la mise en place du réseau des instituts "Jazari", une plateforme d’instituts d’excellence ancrés dans les douze régions du Royaume”

    “Le noyau technique et stratégique de ce réseau repose sur l’institut "JAZARI ROOT" autour de trois thématiques clés, à savoir l’E-GOV, les smart zones rurales et montagneuses, ainsi que les grands événements sportifs.”

  • MAI-EV-0008VERIFIED FACT

    Signature of the constitutive convention of the public-interest group "Institut Jazari ROOT"

    On 12 January 2026, at the official launch of the "AI Made in Morocco" programme organised by the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform, the president of CGEM signed the constitutive convention of the public-interest group (GIP) "Institut Jazari ROOT" alongside several ministerial departments and public institutions.

    “Casablanca, le 12 janvier 2026 - La Confédération Générale des Entreprises du Maroc (CGEM) a pris part à la cérémonie officielle de lancement du programme “AI Made in Morocco – L’IA au cœur de la transformation numérique et des services publics”, organisée par le Ministère de la Transition Numérique et de la Réforme de l’Administration.”

    “a procédé à la signature de la convention constitutive du Groupement d’Intérêt Public (GIP) “Institut Jazari ROOT”, aux côtés de plusieurs départements ministériels et d’institutions publiques”
