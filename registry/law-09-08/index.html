    CNDP lists Law 09-08 in its regulatory framework

    The website of the CNDP (cndp.ma), Morocco's national commission for the control of personal data protection, lists Law 09-08 under "Cadre règlementaire" (regulatory framework) in its "À propos de la CNDP" section, alongside the Constitution and Decree No. 2-09-165, and has a dedicated page for the law.
