02Evidence

  • MAI-EV-0025VERIFIED FACT

    Al-Atlas-0.5B model card

    Al-Atlas-0.5B is published on Hugging Face by the atlasia organisation as a 0.5B-parameter language model trained on Moroccan Darija, fine-tuned from Qwen2.5-0.5B on a curated 155M-token Darija corpus.

    “Al-Atlas is a 0.5B parameter language model specifically trained on Moroccan Darija”

    “The model was finetuned from Qwen-2.5 and trained on a carefully curated dataset of 155M tokens, focusing exclusively on authentic Moroccan Darija content.”

    “Base model Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B”
