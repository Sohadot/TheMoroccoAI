Idarati AI

Agentic AI assistant for administrative procedures, presented by the Ministry of Digital Transition in September 2026. On 18 October 2026 it was publicly available on the ministry's own services: listed on its AI Marketplace, opened from an "Assistant Idarati" button on the national procedures portal idarati.ma, and answering a test question at chatbot.idarati.ma.

02Evidence

  • MAI-EV-0040VERIFIED FACT

    Maroc IA 2030: presentation of the roadmap's "main advances"

    The national portal reports that the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform presented in Rabat what it calls the main advances of the Maroc IA 2030 roadmap. The article describes the roadmap as resting on three pillars and ten structuring programmes, and lists the items presented: the sovereign AI marketplace, Idarati AI, a proof of concept of the national meta-application and e-wallet, and a "Livre Rose" on digital sovereignty.

    “a présenté, mardi à Rabat, les principales avancées de la feuille de route "Maroc IA 2030"”

    “Cette feuille de route repose sur trois grands socles”

    “outre dix programmes structurants”

    “Parmi les premières solutions figure Idarati AI”

    “un Proof of Concept (POC) de la méta-application et du e-wallet national”

  • MAI-EV-0013ANNOUNCED PLAN

    Sovereign AI marketplace and the Idarati AI assistant presented

    The ministry unveiled a sovereign AI marketplace intended to give access to secure and trusted AI solutions hosted under Moroccan control, and presented Idarati AI, an agentic AI assistant intended to help users with administrative procedures.

    “The ministry also unveiled a sovereign AI marketplace designed to provide access to secure and trusted AI solutions while ensuring data protection and maintaining control over infrastructure in Morocco.”

    “One of the first solutions presented was Idarati AI, an agentic AI assistant intended to inform, guide and assist users with administrative procedures.”

  • MAI-EV-0038VERIFIED FACT

    Assistant Citoyen at chatbot.idarati.ma: live service observation

    The citizen assistant at chatbot.idarati.ma, titled "Assistant Citoyen — Royaume du Maroc" and headed with the name of the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform, was publicly accessible without an account on the retrieval date. It presents itself as an automated assistant giving official administrative information and warns that its answers may contain errors.

    “Assistant Citoyen — Royaume du Maroc”

    “Ministère de la Transition Numérique”

    “Information administrative officielle”

    “Assistant automatisé. Les réponses peuvent contenir des erreurs — vérifiez les informations essentielles auprès des services concernés”

  • MAI-EV-0035VERIFIED FACT

    Idarati national procedures portal embedding the Assistant Idarati chatbot

    The national portal of administrative procedures (idarati.ma), whose copyright line names the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform, carried a chatbot component titled "Assistant Idarati" on the retrieval date. The portal's interface text for that component includes the label "Powered by Berexia" and a disclaimer that the chatbot's answers are for information only.

    “Copyright 2024 © Ministère de la Transition Numérique et de la Réforme de l'Administration”
