    DeepEcho website: product description and regulatory disclaimer

    DeepEcho describes its software as using AI and deep learning to help radiologists and minimally trained clinicians with ultrasound video diagnosis, and states on its website that its claims and metrics are not yet validated by the FDA and come from its own clinical studies under scientific and regulatory review.

    “Deepecho uses Artificial Intelligence and Deep Learning to mimic what a trained sonographer would do and help radiologists and minimally trained clinicians with ultrasound video diagnosis.”

    “Claims, figures, metrics & any kind of scientific and / or marketing materials listed in the current website are not validated yet by the FDA.”

    “Metrics comes from our own clinical studies (currently under scientific & regulatory review)”

    The quotation still matches its source, but the claim is no longer current; see MAI-EV-0032, MAI-EV-0039.

    The disclaimer is quoted as the site showed it on the retrieval date, but it appears outdated: the FDA database lists 510(k) clearance K242594 for a DEEPECHO device on 23 May 2025 (MAI-EV-0032, MAI-EV-0039), and the Moroccan government says DeepEcho's solution obtained FDA "approbation" (MAI-EV-0033). It is therefore no longer used to establish a lifecycle stage: a quotation that still matches its source can support a claim that is no longer current. Accuracy and speed figures on the site are self-reported and not recorded.
