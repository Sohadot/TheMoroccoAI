  • MAI-EV-0013ANNOUNCED PLAN

    Sovereign AI marketplace and the Idarati AI assistant presented

    The ministry unveiled a sovereign AI marketplace intended to give access to secure and trusted AI solutions hosted under Moroccan control, and presented Idarati AI, an agentic AI assistant intended to help users with administrative procedures.

    “The ministry also unveiled a sovereign AI marketplace designed to provide access to secure and trusted AI solutions while ensuring data protection and maintaining control over infrastructure in Morocco.”

    “One of the first solutions presented was Idarati AI, an agentic AI assistant intended to inform, guide and assist users with administrative procedures.”

  • MAI-EV-0034VERIFIED FACT

    Ministry AI Marketplace portal listing Idarati AI

    The ministry's AI Marketplace portal (ai.mmsp.gov.ma) was publicly accessible on the retrieval date. It describes itself as the ministry's unified portal of secure and trusted AI solutions, and lists "Idarati AI" as an agentic AI assistant for public services, flagged as a flagship service and pointing to chatbot.idarati.ma.
