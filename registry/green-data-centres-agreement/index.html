    Synthesis report: agreement on sovereign green data-centre infrastructure

    Among the conventions signed at the First National AI Conference was an agreement with the Ministry of Energy Transition and Sustainable Development on establishing sustainable, high-performing and sovereign digital infrastructures (green data centres).

    “the Minister Delegate for the Head of Government in Charge of Digital Transition and Administrative Reform officially signed several important agreements and memoranda during the event or at the closing ceremony.”

    “The Ministry of Energy Transition and Sustainable Development: For the establishment and development of sustainable, high-performing, and sovereign digital infrastructures, in line with energy and climate challenges (Green Data Centers).”

  • MAI-EV-0019VERIFIED FACT

    Agreements signed in Salé on the sidelines of the First National AI Conference

    On 2 July 2025 in Salé, on the sidelines of the First National AI Conference, the Ministry of Digital Transition signed: a memorandum for an Al Jazari Institute centre of excellence in Nador with the Minister of Higher Education, Scientific Research and Innovation and Oriental Region authorities; a data-centre partnership memorandum with the President of UM6P; and an agreement with the Minister of Energy Transition and Sustainable Development.

    “Several agreements were signed, on Wednesday in Salé, during a ceremony held on the sidelines of the First National Conference on AI.”

    “A MoU for the creation of an "Al Jazari Institute" Center of Excellence in the province of Nador was also signed between Seghrouchni and the Minister of Higher Education, Scientific Research and Innovation, Azzedine El Midaoui, the Wali of the Oriental Region, Khatib El Hebil, the governor of the province of Nador, Jamal Chaarani and the President of the Regional Council, Mohamed Bouarourou.”

    “a MoU marking the launch of a strategic partnership in the field of data centers was signed between Seghrouchni and the President of Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P), Hicham El Habti.”

    “An agreement was also signed between the Minister in charge of Digital Transition and Administrative Reform and the Minister of Energy Transition and Sustainable Development, Leila Benali.”
