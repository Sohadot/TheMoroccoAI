Switch Al Maghrib (SWAM)

SWAM

Operator of Morocco's national card-payment switch, which describes itself as one of the country's five financial market infrastructures, licensed and supervised by Bank Al-Maghrib. In March 2026 it announced it will integrate an AI-based fraud scoring solution from Visa.

  • MAI-EV-0036ANNOUNCED PLAN

    Switch Al Maghrib to integrate Visa's AI-based real-time fraud scoring

    Switch Al Maghrib's press release of 26 March 2026 says SWAM will integrate Visa Advanced Authorization (Network Agnostic VAA), an AI-based real-time fraud risk scoring solution applied at authorisation, into its security system for domestic card transactions processed through the national switch.

    “SWAM intégrera la solution Visa Advanced Authorization (Network Agnostic VAA) à son dispositif de sécurité, renforçant ainsi la surveillance de la fraude pour les transactions domestiques par carte traitées via le switch national.”

    “fournit un scoring de risque de fraude en temps réel, basé sur l’intelligence artificielle, au moment de l’autorisation”
