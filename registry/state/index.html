07Evidence transparency

Each record carries one classification, which describes what its source documents. The lists below show where the registry's evidence stops short of a verified fact. They are boundaries of the evidence, not judgments of the records or of their sources.

VERIFIED FACTAn event, act or status that the cited source documents directly (for example, a signature, a launch or an appointment).
OFFICIAL TARGETA quantified goal set by an official body. It is not an outcome and must not be read as achieved.
ANNOUNCED PLANA programme, institution or product an official body has announced or presented, whose operational delivery the registry has not verified.
INDEPENDENT ANALYSISAn inference drawn by TheMoroccoAI from two or more records. It is labelled as interpretation, not as fact.
UNVERIFIED CLAIMA statement attributed to a source whose underlying data the registry has not independently checked.
