  • MAI-EV-0042VERIFIED FACT

    GemMaroc: the preprint describing the GemMaroc fine-tuning runs

    The preprint introducing the GemMaroc Moroccan Darija language models, by three authors from Mohammed VI Polytechnic University's College of Computing, one from Hugging Face and one from the National Institute of Posts and Telecoms, reports that its fine-tuning experiments ran on two NVIDIA A100-80GB GPUs, and the 27-billion-parameter GemMaroc model on eight NVIDIA H100-80GB GPUs, taking about 48 GPU-hours. It expresses the total cost as a cloud cost, using a public on-demand rate in a Runpod Cloud region, and does not name the computing facility used.

    “College of Computing, Mohammed VI Polytechnic University, Benguerir, Morocco”

    “All fine-tuning experiments were conducted on 2×A100-80GB GPUs, except for our largest model, the 27-billion-parameter GEMMAROC, which was fine-tuned on 8×H100-80GB GPUs.”

    “consuming approximately 48 GPU-hours and keeping the total cloud cost well under $100.”

    “Based on the public on-demand rate of $2 per A100-hour in the selected Runpod Cloud region.”

    arXiv preprint, version 1 of 20 May 2025, not peer-reviewed; excerpts matched against the PDF. The excerpt on the hardware was checked against the rendered page: the PDF's text layer splits the small-capital model name and drops the hyphen in "H100-80GB" at a line break, so automated matching reports it missing. No author is named Belkasmi: the Toubkal article (MAI-EV-0041) attributes these runs to "Belkasmi et al." but its reference [57] is this preprint by Skiredj et al. The preprint does not mention Toubkal, the African Supercomputing Centre or any acknowledgement of computing resources; its cost footnote uses a commercial cloud provider's public rate, which may be a costing convention rather than where the runs took place. It therefore neither confirms nor rules out that the runs were made on Toubkal. Three of the five authors are affiliated with the university that runs Toubkal.

  • MAI-EV-0025VERIFIED FACT

    Al-Atlas-0.5B model card

    Al-Atlas-0.5B is published on Hugging Face by the atlasia organisation as a 0.5B-parameter language model trained on Moroccan Darija, fine-tuned from Qwen2.5-0.5B on a curated 155M-token Darija corpus.

    “Al-Atlas is a 0.5B parameter language model specifically trained on Moroccan Darija”

    “The model was finetuned from Qwen-2.5 and trained on a carefully curated dataset of 155M tokens, focusing exclusively on authentic Moroccan Darija content.”

    “Base model Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B”

  • MAI-EV-0021VERIFIED FACT

    Atlas-Chat: Adapting Large Language Models for Low-Resource Moroccan Arabic Dialect (arXiv:2409.17912)

    A preprint first submitted on 26 September 2024 introduces Atlas-Chat, large language models for Moroccan Arabic (Darija) in 2B, 9B and 27B sizes, fine-tuned on an instruction dataset built by consolidating existing Darija resources, creating new manual and synthetic data, and translating English instructions.

    “Atlas-Chat: Adapting Large Language Models for Low-Resource Moroccan Arabic Dialect”

    “Focusing on Moroccan Arabic, also known as Darija, we construct our instruction dataset by consolidating existing Darija language resources, creating novel datasets both manually and synthetically, and translating English instructions with stringent quality control.”

    “Atlas-Chat-2B, 9B, and 27B models, fine-tuned on the dataset”

  • MAI-EV-0022VERIFIED FACT

    Atlas-Chat-9B model card

    Atlas-Chat is published on Hugging Face as a family of open models instruction-tuned for Darija. The 9B model card names MBZUAI France Lab, a Paris research centre affiliated with MBZUAI in Abu Dhabi, as developer; lists the Gemma licence; and gives google/gemma-2-9b-it as the model it was fine-tuned from.

    “Atlas-Chat is a family of open models instruction-tuned for Darija, the colloquial Arabic of Morocco”

    “The model is developed by MBZUAI France Lab, an AI research center in Paris affiliated with the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) headquartered in Abu Dhabi.”

    “License: gemma”

    “Base model google/gemma-2-9b Finetuned google/gemma-2-9b-it”
