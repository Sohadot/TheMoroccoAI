02Evidence

  • MAI-EV-0033VERIFIED FACT

    Ministry of Health convention with DeepEcho on AI prenatal ultrasound in public facilities

    At GITEX Future Health Africa Morocco in May 2026, the Ministry of Health and Social Protection signed a partnership convention with DeepEcho setting up an institutional and research framework for the dissemination, adoption and clinical evaluation of AI solutions for pregnancy-monitoring ultrasound in public health facilities. The source describes a progressive deployment, within an evaluative scientific study, in primary-care establishments and local hospitals, of a Moroccan solution that it says obtained the "approbation" of the US Food and Drug Administration.

    “le ministère a signé une convention de partenariat avec la société "DeepEcho", visant la mise en place d’un cadre institutionnel et de recherche pour la diffusion, l’adoption et l’évaluation clinique de solutions d’IA dans le domaine de l’échographie de suivi de la grossesse au sein des établissements de santé publics”

    “à travers le déploiement progressif, dans le cadre d’une étude scientifique évaluative, d’une solution numérique marocaine innovante, ayant obtenu l’approbation de la Food and Drug Administration Américaine (FDA), au niveau des établissements de soins de santé primaires et des hôpitaux de proximité”

  • MAI-EV-0039VERIFIED FACT

    FDA 510(k) K242594: clearance letter and 510(k) summary for DEEPECHO

    The FDA's letter of 23 May 2025, addressed to DeepEcho at a Dover, Delaware address, determines that the DEEPECHO device is substantially equivalent to legally marketed predicate devices for the stated indications for use, so that the company may market it subject to general controls. The 510(k) summary describes DEEPECHO as cloud-based software that uses machine learning to detect standard fetal biometry views and assist in computing fetal biometric measurements, for trained healthcare professionals; gives a contact e-mail address on the deepecho.io domain; and states that training data came in part from sites in Morocco and test data in part from Casablanca.

    “have determined the device is substantially equivalent (for the indications for use stated in the enclosure)”

    “that do not require approval of a premarket approval application (PMA). You may, therefore, market the device, subject to the general controls”

    “DEEPECHO is intended to analyze fetal ultrasound images and clips using machine learning techniques to detect standard biometry views.”

    “DEEPECHO is a cloud-based standalone software as a medical device (SaMD)”

    “deepecho.io”

    “Training data were obtained from two Roshan MFM clinics in New York City and selected sites in Morocco.”

    “Casablanca (Morocco)”
