Registry / Evidence gaps

MAI-GAPS — Open evidence questions

Evidence gap register

Questions this registry cannot yet answer, with what it does establish, what it does not, and what evidence would settle each one.

A gap is a limit of this registry's coverage. The statement "No qualifying evidence is currently recorded in this registry" does not mean that no such evidence exists, nor that anything failed. Machine-readable: gaps.json.

Open evidence questions. A gap records what this registry establishes and what it does not; it is a limit of the registry's coverage, not a finding that something does not exist. A gap is opened when a reference question asks something the records cannot answer; every strategy with an official target has a gap on its measured progress, which the build checks. Each gap stores the records it was assessed against and a digest of the data it rests on: when that data changes (a new or edited record, a changed stage, attribute or relationship), the build fails until the gap is re-assessed. Checks are re-run on every build against the canonical data.

GAP-01What documents actual use, adoption, accuracy, service outcomes or sustained operation of Idarati AI?

Open Last assessed 19 October 2026 · Concerns: Idarati AI

What this registry establishes

  • The ministry presented Idarati AI as an agentic AI assistant for administrative procedures. MAI-EV-0013 MAI-EV-0040
  • It was publicly available on the ministry's own services on 18 October 2026, and answered a test question through its public interface. MAI-EV-0034 MAI-EV-0035 MAI-EV-0038

What it does not establish

How many people use it, its accuracy, its effect on administrative services, and whether it has operated continuously. Public availability on one date does not establish any of these. No qualifying evidence is currently recorded in this registry.

Evidence that would resolve it

  • An operator's report of use (annual report, parliamentary answer, published usage statistics)
  • An independent evaluation of the service

GAP-02What publicly documented measurements show progress toward the official targets of Morocco AI 2030?

Open Last assessed 19 October 2026 · Concerns: Morocco AI 2030

What this registry establishes

  • The roadmap sets three 2030 targets: MAD 100 billion in added value to GDP, 50,000 direct and indirect jobs, and 200,000 trained and certified AI talents. MAI-EV-0011
  • The ministry's September 2026 presentation of the roadmap's "main advances" restated the targets and listed items presented (the AI marketplace, Idarati AI, a proof of concept of a national meta-application and e-wallet), without quantified progress towards the targets. MAI-EV-0040
  • Two projects are recorded as included in the roadmap, on the basis of that presentation. MAI-EV-0013

What it does not establish

Any measured progress towards the value-added, jobs or talent targets. A project's status is not a measure of progress towards them. No qualifying evidence is currently recorded in this registry.

Evidence that would resolve it

  • Official indicator reporting on the targets (ministry progress report, budget documents, national statistics)

GAP-03What independently documents AI workloads on the Toubkal supercomputer, and at what scale?

Open Last assessed 19 October 2026 · Concerns: Toubkal supercomputer

What this registry establishes

  • Toubkal is listed in the June 2026 TOP500 as general-purpose high-performance computing; the listing documents capacity, not workloads or users. MAI-EV-0020
  • A peer-reviewed article by authors affiliated with its host university states that large-language-model fine-tuning has been run on it, and presents as a benchmark the runs that produced the GemMaroc Moroccan Darija models. MAI-EV-0041
  • The GemMaroc preprint that the article cites (Skiredj et al.; three of its five authors are from the host university) reports the hardware used, A100 GPUs and, for its largest model, eight H100 GPUs, and about 48 GPU-hours for that longest run, but does not say where the runs were made, and expresses their cost at a commercial cloud provider's public rate. MAI-EV-0042
  • Two researchers of the host university report training a bacterial genomic language model on Toubkal: two A100 GPUs for 37 hours. MAI-EV-0043

What it does not establish

Independent documentation of AI workloads on Toubkal, and the scale, share, users or continuity of its AI use. This is not a finding that Toubkal is not used for AI: researchers affiliated with its operator report specific runs. What is missing is documentation by a party independent of the operator, and system-level, quantified reporting. No qualifying evidence is currently recorded in this registry.

Evidence that would resolve it

  • Published usage or allocation reports for Toubkal (projects, GPU-hours, AI share)
  • Publications by authors not affiliated with UM6P that acknowledge Toubkal for specific AI models
  • A statement by the GemMaroc authors of where their fine-tuning runs were made

GAP-06What publicly documented measurements show progress toward the official targets of Digital Morocco 2030?

Open Last assessed 20 October 2026 · Concerns: Digital Morocco 2030

What this registry establishes

  • The strategy was launched in Rabat on 25 September 2024 by the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform. MAI-EV-0001
  • Its official 2030 objectives include 240,000 direct jobs with a contribution to GDP, digitalised public services, and a rise from 113th to 50th place worldwide on the ranking of online public services. The strategy's brochure defines that ranking as the United Nations Online Services Index (OSI), a component of the e-government development index (EGDI), and gives 113th as Morocco's 2022 position on it; the national news agency's dispatch calls it the e-government development index. MAI-EV-0002 MAI-EV-0049
  • The Head of Government stated that public investment in digital technology rose from MAD 11 million in 2021 to more than MAD 1.7 billion in 2024. The registry has not checked these figures, and spending is an input, not a measure of the targets. MAI-EV-0003
  • The ministry's report on the First National AI Conference frames the conference within the strategy. MAI-EV-0018
  • On the overall e-government development index, the United Nations ranked Morocco 101st in 2022 and 90th in 2024. On the Online Services Index, its page gives Morocco's value (0.47210 in 2022, 0.56184 in 2024) but not its rank, so the strategy's 113th place for 2022 is the strategy's figure and has not been verified against a United Nations ranking. The 2024 edition, published in the year of the launch, measures the starting position, not progress under the strategy. MAI-EV-0046 MAI-EV-0049 MAI-EV-0002

What it does not establish

Any measured progress, after the strategy's launch, towards the jobs, GDP-contribution, public-services or online-services ranking objectives, and Morocco's rank on the Online Services Index in any edition. No project is recorded as included in the strategy, the investment figure is an unchecked statement about spending, and no edition of the United Nations survey later than 2024 had been published on the assessment date. No qualifying evidence is currently recorded in this registry.

Evidence that would resolve it

  • Official indicator reporting on the targets (ministry progress report, budget documents, national statistics)
  • The United Nations ranking of countries on the Online Services Index, the index the ranking objective refers to, for 2022 and later editions
  • Finance-law documents for the stated investment figures

GAP-07Which JAZARI institutes have documented governance, staff, premises, operational activity or outputs, beyond their founding agreements and legal approval?

Open Last assessed 20 October 2026 · Concerns: Institut Jazari ROOT, JAZARI EduTech, JAZARI Industrie X.0, JAZARI Smart City, Al Jazari Institute, Province of Nador

What this registry establishes

  • The founding conventions of JAZARI ROOT, JAZARI EduTech, JAZARI Industrie X.0 and JAZARI Smart City were approved by joint orders published in Bulletin Officiel 7534 of 13 August 2026; the gazette publishes summaries of the conventions, not their signed texts. MAI-EV-0047
  • The ministry states that the general assemblies of these four institutes were held in Rabat on 8 September 2026, chaired by the minister; the release names no director, board, budget, staff or premises. MAI-EV-0048
  • A memorandum of understanding for an Al Jazari institute in the Province of Nador was signed on 2 July 2025. MAI-EV-0005 MAI-EV-0019

What it does not establish

For each institute: staff, premises in use, operational activity and its outputs, a documented decision or statement that operation has not begun, or a documented end of activity after operation, so each institute's operational status is unknown in this registry. For the Nador institute, also any legal act establishing it and any governance event. Legal approval, a general assembly, or a ministry's description of a move to operational implementation is not operation. No qualifying evidence is currently recorded in this registry.

Evidence that would resolve it

  • For each institute, records of staff or premises in use, together with dated activity carried out by the institute and its outputs, from the institute itself or from an official or independent source
  • Or, for an institute, a dated record that it has not begun operating or will not begin, or a record that it stopped operating after its activity began
  • For the Nador institute, the legal act establishing it, if one has been adopted

Assessment by institute

A gap may assess each of its entities separately (entity_assessments). Each assessment lists, for every evidence dimension, the records that document it for that entity; an empty list means no qualifying record is recorded in this registry. An entity is resolved when its records meet one of the resolution paths below, which document that it operates, that it has not commenced operating, or that it stopped after operating; an entity that meets none has an unknown operational status and stays open; it stays in the gap's scope, marked resolved, while other entities remain open. The gap is resolved only when every entity is.

  • Legal approval: A legal act establishing the entity, such as a joint order approving its founding convention.
  • Governance event: A meeting of a governing body, or the appointment of a named officer or board member.
  • Staffing: Staff recruited or in post, named or counted.
  • Premises: Premises opened, occupied or in use.
  • Operational activity: Dated activity carried out by the entity itself: programmes run, calls launched, services delivered.
  • Outputs: Dated outputs of that activity: projects funded, people trained, systems or publications delivered.
  • Documented non-commencement: A dated record that the entity has not begun operating, or will not begin (cancelled or abandoned before any activity). It states the entity's status at that record's date; it is never inferred from missing records.
  • Documented discontinuation: A record that the entity stopped operating, or was dissolved, after its activity had begun.

A record cited under a dimension must be a VERIFIED FACT whose related_entities include the entity. Announced plans, targets and descriptions of intent do not qualify for any dimension. An institute resolves when it is documented as operating (operational_activity and outputs each cite at least one record, staffing or premises cites at least one record, and the entity's lifecycle is pilot or operational under the registry's status rules), as not having commenced operation (documented_non_commencement cites at least one record, operational_activity and outputs cite none, and the entity's lifecycle is not pilot, operational or discontinued. No stage change is required: the registry's lifecycle vocabulary has no stage for an entity that never began operating, and discontinued is not used for one), or as discontinued after operating (documented_discontinuation and operational_activity each cite at least one record, and the entity's lifecycle is discontinued. An entity that stopped before any activity meets not_commenced instead); otherwise its operational status is unknown and it stays open. A legal approval or a governance event never resolves an entity on its own, and missing records never do: an entity that meets no path has an unknown operational status and stays open. Records of both non-commencement and activity for the same entity conflict and must be re-assessed. An entity marked resolved must meet a path and list the records that meet it in resolved_by; an entity that meets a path but is still marked open must be re-assessed. A resolution records the status at the date of its records; a later record concerning the entity requires the gap to be re-assessed.
