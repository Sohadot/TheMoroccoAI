MAI-GAPS — Open evidence questions

Evidence gap register

Questions this registry cannot yet answer, with what it does establish, what it does not, and what evidence would settle each one.

A gap is a limit of this registry's coverage. The statement "No qualifying evidence is currently recorded in this registry" does not mean that no such evidence exists, nor that anything failed. Machine-readable: gaps.json.

Open evidence questions. A gap records what this registry establishes and what it does not; it is a limit of the registry's coverage, not a finding that something does not exist. Each gap stores the records it was assessed against and a digest of the data it rests on: when that data changes (a new or edited record, a changed stage, attribute or relationship), the build fails until the gap is re-assessed. Checks are re-run on every build against the canonical data.

GAP-01What documents actual use, adoption, accuracy, service outcomes or sustained operation of Idarati AI?

Open Last assessed 19 October 2026 · Concerns: Idarati AI

What this registry establishes

  • The ministry presented Idarati AI as an agentic AI assistant for administrative procedures. MAI-EV-0013 MAI-EV-0040
  • It was publicly available on the ministry's own services on 18 October 2026, and answered a test question through its public interface. MAI-EV-0034 MAI-EV-0035 MAI-EV-0038

What it does not establish

How many people use it, its accuracy, its effect on administrative services, and whether it has operated continuously. Public availability on one date does not establish any of these. No qualifying evidence is currently recorded in this registry.

Evidence that would resolve it

  • An operator's report of use (annual report, parliamentary answer, published usage statistics)
  • An independent evaluation of the service

GAP-02What publicly documented measurements show progress toward the official targets of Morocco AI 2030?

Open Last assessed 19 October 2026 · Concerns: Morocco AI 2030

What this registry establishes

  • The roadmap sets three 2030 targets: MAD 100 billion in added value to GDP, 50,000 direct and indirect jobs, and 200,000 trained and certified AI talents. MAI-EV-0011
  • The ministry's September 2026 presentation of the roadmap's "main advances" restated the targets and listed items presented (the AI marketplace, Idarati AI, a proof of concept of a national meta-application and e-wallet), without quantified progress towards the targets. MAI-EV-0040
  • Two projects are recorded as included in the roadmap, on the basis of that presentation. MAI-EV-0013

What it does not establish

Any measured progress towards the value-added, jobs or talent targets. A project's status is not a measure of progress towards them. No qualifying evidence is currently recorded in this registry.

Evidence that would resolve it

  • Official indicator reporting on the targets (ministry progress report, budget documents, national statistics)

GAP-03What independently documents AI workloads on the Toubkal supercomputer, and at what scale?

Open Last assessed 19 October 2026 · Concerns: Toubkal supercomputer

What this registry establishes

  • Toubkal is listed in the June 2026 TOP500 as general-purpose high-performance computing; the listing documents capacity, not workloads or users. MAI-EV-0020
  • A peer-reviewed article by authors affiliated with its host university states that large-language-model fine-tuning has been run on it, and presents as a benchmark the runs that produced the GemMaroc Moroccan Darija models (attributed to Belkasmi et al.). MAI-EV-0041

What it does not establish

Independent documentation of AI workloads on Toubkal, and the scale, share, users or continuity of AI use. This is not a finding that Toubkal is not used for AI: its operator's own authors report specific fine-tuning runs. What is missing is independent and quantified documentation. No qualifying evidence is currently recorded in this registry.

Evidence that would resolve it

  • The GemMaroc study by Belkasmi et al., which the article cites, if it documents its use of Toubkal
  • Published usage or allocation reports
  • Independent publications acknowledging Toubkal for specific AI models

GAP-04Which announced or signed computing and data-centre initiatives have verifiable construction, commissioning or operation?

Open Last assessed 19 October 2026 · Concerns: Nexus AI Factory, Sovereign green data-centre infrastructure agreement, UM6P data-centre partnership convention

What this registry establishes

  • Agreements on green data-centre infrastructure and on a data-centre partnership with UM6P were signed at the First National AI Conference. MAI-EV-0006 MAI-EV-0007 MAI-EV-0019
  • A memorandum on the Nexus AI Factory is reported by the press only. MAI-EV-0030

What it does not establish

Construction, commissioning or operation of any of these facilities. No qualifying evidence is currently recorded in this registry.

Evidence that would resolve it

  • Official project documentation (permits, commissioning notices)
  • Operator or independent confirmation that a facility is built and running

GAP-05Which relationships asserting a formal act (a signature, creation, designation, investment, development or operation) rest only on announcements, news reports or descriptions in this registry?

Open Last assessed 19 October 2026 · Concerns: Relationships listed below

What this registry establishes

  • See the relationships listed below.

What it does not establish

The instrument or formal record behind each relationship listed below: the signed text, founding act, designation, investment or operating record. This measures how each relationship is documented in this registry, not whether it is true: an official report of a signing is good evidence that a signing was announced, not of what the instrument says. No qualifying evidence is currently recorded in this registry.

Evidence that would resolve it

  • Published texts of the conventions and memoranda
  • Official Bulletin publications where applicable
