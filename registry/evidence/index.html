  • MAI-EV-0041VERIFIED FACT

    TOUBKAL: peer-reviewed description of the system and its reported workloads

    A peer-reviewed article in The Journal of Supercomputing, by authors affiliated with Mohammed VI Polytechnic University's College of Computing, describes Toubkal's GPU partitions (NVIDIA A100 nodes from the initial 2022 deployment and H100 nodes added in 2025). It states that fine-tuning of transformer-based large language models and large-scale computational fluid dynamics simulations have been run on the system, and presents as a benchmark the fine-tuning runs, attributed to Belkasmi et al., that produced the GemMaroc Moroccan Darija language models.

    “TOUBKAL: a high-performance supercomputer powering scientific research in Africa”

    “The initial deployment in 2022 included five Dell XE8545 nodes with dual AMD EPYC 7713 CPUs and four NVIDIA A100-SXM4-80GB GPUs per node.”

    “In 2025, three Dell XE8640 nodes were added, each with dual Intel Xeon Platinum 8468 CPUs and four NVIDIA H100-HBM3-80GB GPUs.”

    “we examine practical workloads such as fine-tuning transformer-based large language models (LLMs) and conducting large-scale computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations, both of which have been successfully run on Toubkal.”

    “we present a practical benchmark demonstrating the fine-tuning of a large language model (LLM) performed on the Toubkal supercomputer by Belkasmi et al.”

    “This work led to the creation of GemMaroc”

    Source
    The Journal of Supercomputing (Springer) · Peer-reviewed journal
