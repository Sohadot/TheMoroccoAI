    Morocco AI 2030 roadmap: pillars and the JAZARI Institutes network

    The Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform presented the Morocco AI 2030 roadmap in Rabat in September 2026. As presented, it is structured around three pillars (sovereignty and trust; innovation and competitiveness; impact, adoption and outreach) and relies on the national JAZARI Institutes network, described as comprising JAZARI ROOT, JAZARI Smart City, JAZARI EduTech and JAZARI Industrie X.0.

    “Morocco’s Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform on Tuesday in Rabat presented the main advances under the "Morocco AI 2030" roadmap”

    “The strategy is built around three pillars: sovereignty and trust, innovation and competitiveness, and impact, adoption and outreach.”

    “Its implementation notably relies on the national network of JAZARI Institutes, the operational arm for AI research and innovation, comprising JAZARI ROOT, JAZARI Smart City, JAZARI EduTech and JAZARI Industrie X.0.”

  • MAI-EV-0048VERIFIED FACT

    Ministry release: general assemblies of four JAZARI institutes

    The ministry's release states that the general assemblies of four JAZARI institutes, JAZARI ROOT, the JAZARI institute for education and technological innovation, JAZARI Industrie X.0 and the JAZARI institute for smart cities, were held in Rabat on Tuesday 8 September 2026, chaired by the Minister Delegate for Digital Transition and Administration Reform, after the publication in August 2026 of the decisions approving their founding conventions.

    “Les assemblées générales de quatre Instituts « JAZARI » se sont tenues ce mardi à Rabat, sous la présidence de Madame Amal El Fallah Seghrouchni, ministre déléguée auprès du Chef du Gouvernement, chargée de la Transition numérique et de la Réforme de l’administration.”

    “Il s’agit de JAZARI ROOT, de l’Institut JAZARI pour l’éducation et l’innovation technologique, de l’Institut JAZARI Industrie X.0 et de l’Institut JAZARI des smart cities.”

    “La tenue de ces assemblées générales intervient après une étape institutionnelle majeure, avec la publication au Bulletin officiel, en août 2026, des décisions portant approbation des conventions constitutives de ces instituts.”

    “Cette nouvelle étape marque le passage de ces instituts de la phase de création et de structuration institutionnelle à celle de la mise en œuvre opérationnelle de leurs missions.”

    Bulletin Officiel 7534: joint orders approving the founding conventions of four JAZARI institutes

    Bulletin Officiel no. 7534 of 13 August 2026 publishes four joint orders of the Minister Delegate for Digital Transition and Administration Reform and the Minister Delegate for the Budget approving the founding conventions of four public-interest groups: JAZARI ROOT (order 1535.26 of 16 July 2026) and JAZARI Education and Technological Innovation (1618.26), JAZARI Industrie X.0 (1619.26) and JAZARI Smart City (1620.26), all of 22 July 2026, each followed by a published summary of the convention. The summaries list the president of CGEM among the founding members of JAZARI ROOT, and the Ministries of Economy and Finance, Industry and Commerce, and Digital Transition, with the Euro-Mediterranean University of Fès, Sidi Mohammed Ben Abdellah University, Moulay Ismail University and Al Akhawayn University, as the founding members of JAZARI Industrie X.0.

  • MAI-EV-0009VERIFIED FACT

    Convention signed in Fès creating the Al-Jazari Institute for Industry "JAZARI Industrie X.0"

    On 10 March 2026 in Fès, the Ministry of Industry and Commerce, the Ministry Delegate for Digital Transition and Administration Reform and the Ministry of Economy and Finance signed, with the four universities of the Fès-Meknès region, a convention for the creation of the Al-Jazari Institute for Industry "JAZARI Industrie X.0", whose stated focus is AI, the Internet of Things, advanced robotics and industrial data analytics.

    “Une convention a été signée, mardi 10 Mars à Fès, pour la création de l’Institut Al-Jazari pour l’Industrie "JAZARI Industrie X.0"”

    “La convention a été signée par le ministère de l’Industrie et du Commerce, le ministère délégué chargé de la Transition numérique et de la Réforme de l’administration et le ministère de l’Economie et des Finances, d’une part, et les quatre université que compte la région Fès-Meknès. Il s’agit de l’Université Euro-Méditerranéenne de Fès, l’Université Sidi Mohammed Ben Abdellah de Fès, l’Université Moulay Ismail de Meknès et l’Université Al Akhawayn d’Ifrane”

    “le développement de solutions innovantes reposant sur l’intelligence artificielle, l’Internet des objets, la robotique avancée et l’analyse des données industrielles”
