    Sovereign AI marketplace and the Idarati AI assistant presented

    The ministry unveiled a sovereign AI marketplace intended to give access to secure and trusted AI solutions hosted under Moroccan control, and presented Idarati AI, an agentic AI assistant intended to help users with administrative procedures.

    “The ministry also unveiled a sovereign AI marketplace designed to provide access to secure and trusted AI solutions while ensuring data protection and maintaining control over infrastructure in Morocco.”

    “One of the first solutions presented was Idarati AI, an agentic AI assistant intended to inform, guide and assist users with administrative procedures.”

  • MAI-EV-0010ANNOUNCED PLAN

    JAZARI network announced across the twelve regions; JAZARI ROOT as its core

    In January 2026 the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform announced a network of "Jazari" institutes of excellence anchored in the twelve regions of the Kingdom, with JAZARI ROOT as its technical and strategic core, organised around e-government, smart rural and mountain zones, and major sporting events.

    “l’ annonce, en janvier dernier, par le ministère de la transition numérique et de la réforme de l’administration de la mise en place du réseau des instituts "Jazari", une plateforme d’instituts d’excellence ancrés dans les douze régions du Royaume”

    “Le noyau technique et stratégique de ce réseau repose sur l’institut "JAZARI ROOT" autour de trois thématiques clés, à savoir l’E-GOV, les smart zones rurales et montagneuses, ainsi que les grands événements sportifs.”

    Digital Morocco 2030: stated job and e-government objectives

    Digital Morocco 2030 sets the objectives of 240,000 direct jobs with a contribution to GDP in 2030, digitalising public services, and moving Morocco from 113th to 50th place worldwide in the e-government development index.

    “la création de 240.000 emplois directs et la contribution au produit intérieur brut (PIB) en 2030”

    “digitaliser les services publics. Le but étant de faire passer le Maroc de la 113e à la 50e place mondiale dans l'indice de développement du e-gouvernement”

    United Nations E-Government Development Index: Morocco's rank and value, 2003–2024

    The United Nations E-Government Knowledgebase ranks Morocco 90th of 193 countries in the 2024 E-Government Development Index, with a value of 0.6841, against 101st in 2022. Its ranks in the editions from 2024 back to 2003 are 90, 101, 106, 110, 85, 82, 120, 126, 140, 138, 138 and 131.
