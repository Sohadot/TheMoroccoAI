  • MAI-EV-0057VERIFIED FACT

    Cour des comptes annual report 2023–2024 (main points): digitalised public services at end-2023

    The Cour des comptes' summary of its annual report for 2023–2024 states that the number of digitalised public services reached 605 at the end of 2023, that the share of completely digitalised services did not exceed 23 per cent of the services offered, and that this growth had not significantly simplified administrative procedures for users.

    “le chantier de la transformation digitale peine à avancer, comme en témoigne la faible mise en œuvre des programmes numériques.”

    “Bien que le nombre de services publics digitalisés ait atteint 605 à fin 2023, cette évolution n'a pas eu d'impact significatif sur la simplification des procédures administratives pour les usagers, surtout que le pourcentage de services complètement digitalisés ne dépasse pas 23% du total des services offerts”

    Summary ("Principaux axes") of the annual report of the Cour des comptes, the State's supreme audit institution, which is independent of the ministry; dated November 2024 (PDF created 11 December 2024), printed page 13. The verified fact is what the Court states. The summary does not set out its method here (how services were counted, or what "complètement digitalisés" means), and the report's full chapter was not read. The figures describe the position at the end of 2023, before Digital Morocco 2030 was launched in September 2024: they are a pre-strategy baseline, not a measure of progress under the strategy.

  • MAI-EV-0002OFFICIAL TARGET

    Digital Morocco 2030: stated job and e-government objectives

    Digital Morocco 2030 sets the objectives of 240,000 direct jobs with a contribution to GDP in 2030, digitalising public services, and moving Morocco from 113th to 50th place worldwide in what the dispatch calls the e-government development index.

    “la création de 240.000 emplois directs et la contribution au produit intérieur brut (PIB) en 2030”

    “digitaliser les services publics. Le but étant de faire passer le Maroc de la 113e à la 50e place mondiale dans l'indice de développement du e-gouvernement”

    The source does not give a separate target year for the e-government ranking objective; 2030 is the strategy horizon. The strategy's own brochure (MAI-EV-0049) defines the ranking as the United Nations Online Services Index (OSI), a component of the e-government development index (EGDI), and gives 113th as Morocco's 2022 position on it; describing it as the overall index is the dispatch's wording. The 113th place is the strategy's figure: the United Nations page recorded here gives Morocco's OSI values but not its OSI rank, so the registry has not verified it. On the overall EGDI, Morocco ranked 101st in 2022 and 90th in 2024 (MAI-EV-0046).

    United Nations E-Government Development Index and Online Service Index: Morocco, 2003–2024

    The United Nations E-Government Knowledgebase ranks Morocco 90th of 193 countries in the 2024 E-Government Development Index, with a value of 0.6841, against 101st in 2022. Its ranks in the editions from 2024 back to 2003 are 90, 101, 106, 110, 85, 82, 120, 126, 140, 138, 138 and 131.
