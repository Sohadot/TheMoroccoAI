    DarijaBERT model card

    DarijaBERT is published on Hugging Face as a joint release of AIOX Lab and the SI2M Lab of INSEA: a model for written Moroccan Darija based on the BERT-base architecture without next-sentence-prediction pre-training, trained on about 3 million Darija sequences (691MB of text, about 100M tokens).

    “AIOX Lab and SI2M Lab INSEA have joined forces”

    “It is based on the same architecture as BERT-base, but without the Next Sentence Prediction (NSP) objective.”

    “This model was trained on a total of ~3 Million sequences of Darija dialect representing 691MB of text or a total of ~100M tokens.”
