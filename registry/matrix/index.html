Institutional evidence matrix

What each organisation or initiative recorded here is documented as doing with projects, initiatives, instruments and models, with the classification of each relationship, the status of the related entity and the records behind it.

Scope. This matrix covers the 43 relationships recorded in this registry between 24 organisations and initiatives and what they act on. It is not a complete map of Morocco's AI ecosystem, and an absent relationship is a gap in coverage. Machine-readable: matrix.json.

01How to read it

  • Documented as says only what the relationship's classification supports: Signed: 18 · Documented: 12 · Announced or planned: 6 · Developed: 4 · Operated: 2 · Registry inference: 1. "Signed" means the signature is documented, not that anything was delivered; "Operated" means a verified operator; "Announced or planned" is not verified as delivered. For none of the 18 "Signed" rows is the signed text recorded: each signing is documented by a report of it (news dispatch, official announcement, official report, press article, press release), and 8 rows also cite an official gazette record of the instrument's legal approval, which is neither the signed text nor a report of the signing, as the evidence column shows.
  • Status of the related entity is its own lifecycle stage, with its basis. A project's status is not a measure of progress toward any national target.
  • Of the 33 relationships that assert a formal act (a signature, creation, designation, investment, development or operation), 11 rest only on announcements, news reports or descriptions (GAP-05); the others also cite another kind of record, such as an official report. This describes how they are documented in this registry, not whether they are true. Declarations and public events (presented, launched, organised, a programme's stated composition) are not measured this way: an official announcement is their primary record.
  • An association between organisations is not a legal identity or a formal partnership unless a record says so.
