MBZUAI France Lab

MBZUAI-Paris

Paris-based AI research centre affiliated with the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (Abu Dhabi), named as the developer of the Atlas-Chat Darija models. It is not a Moroccan institution; its link to Morocco in this registry is the language it models.

    Darija-SFT-Mixture is published on Hugging Face as an instruction dataset of 458K samples in Darija, released under the ODC-BY licence with the note that some subsets carry non-commercial terms.

  • MAI-EV-0022VERIFIED FACT

    Atlas-Chat-9B model card

    Atlas-Chat is published on Hugging Face as a family of open models instruction-tuned for Darija. The 9B model card names MBZUAI France Lab, a Paris research centre affiliated with MBZUAI in Abu Dhabi, as developer; lists the Gemma licence; and gives google/gemma-2-9b-it as the model it was fine-tuned from.

    “Atlas-Chat is a family of open models instruction-tuned for Darija, the colloquial Arabic of Morocco”

    “The model is developed by MBZUAI France Lab, an AI research center in Paris affiliated with the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) headquartered in Abu Dhabi.”

    “License: gemma”

    “Base model google/gemma-2-9b Finetuned google/gemma-2-9b-it”
