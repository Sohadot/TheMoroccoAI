01Start here

Questions the registry answers lists which organisations have documented AI activity, which projects are operational or only announced, which models and datasets are public, and which relationships are verified. Each answer links to its evidence.

The fullest chain in this version runs from Morocco AI 2030 to the JAZARI Institutes network, its core institute Institut Jazari ROOT and the JAZARI Industrie X.0 convention signed in Fès. It then goes on to the signatories, the stated technologies and the targeted sectors. Each page shows the source excerpt, the publication and retrieval dates, and the classification of every claim.

02How claims are classified

VERIFIED FACTAn event, act or status that the cited source documents directly (for example, a signature, a launch or an appointment).25
OFFICIAL TARGETA quantified goal set by an official body. It is not an outcome and must not be read as achieved.2
ANNOUNCED PLANA programme, institution or product an official body has announced or presented, whose operational delivery the registry has not verified.3
INDEPENDENT ANALYSISAn inference drawn by TheMoroccoAI from two or more records. It is labelled as interpretation, not as fact.2
UNVERIFIED CLAIMA statement attributed to a source whose underlying data the registry has not independently checked.1

Counts cover evidence records, plus relationships for independent analysis. An official target is never shown as an outcome. A record marked “partial” states what could not be checked.

06Method and limits

Each record was retrieved from its source on the stated retrieval date, and its excerpt was matched against the retrieved text. An automated validator checks every build. It requires unique identifiers, https source URLs, valid dates and complete fields, and that every entity and relationship cites existing evidence. It also blocks forward-looking wording in records classified as fact, and rejects any relationship whose classification its evidence does not support.

This version is deliberately small. It does not yet cover AI companies, models or datasets, and it omits facts it could not verify against an original source. Corrections can be sent to agent@sohadot.com.
