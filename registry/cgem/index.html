    Bulletin Officiel 7534: joint orders approving the founding conventions of four JAZARI institutes

    Bulletin Officiel no. 7534 of 13 August 2026 publishes four joint orders of the Minister Delegate for Digital Transition and Administration Reform and the Minister Delegate for the Budget approving the founding conventions of four public-interest groups: JAZARI ROOT (order 1535.26 of 16 July 2026) and JAZARI Education and Technological Innovation (1618.26), JAZARI Industrie X.0 (1619.26) and JAZARI Smart City (1620.26), all of 22 July 2026, each followed by a published summary of the convention. The summaries list the president of CGEM among the founding members of JAZARI ROOT, and the Ministries of Economy and Finance, Industry and Commerce, and Digital Transition, with the Euro-Mediterranean University of Fès, Sidi Mohammed Ben Abdellah University, Moulay Ismail University and Al Akhawayn University, as the founding members of JAZARI Industrie X.0.

    “قرار مشترك للوزيرة المنتدبة لدى رئيس الحكومة المكلفة بالانتقال الرقمي وإصلاح الإدارة والوزير المنتدب لدى وزيرة الاقتصاد والمالية المكلف بالميزانية رقم 1535.26 صادر في فاتح صفر 1448 (16 يوليو 2026) يقضي بالمصادقة على الاتفاقية المؤسسة للمجموعة ذات النفع العام المسماة «معهد جزري النواة».”

    “ينشر هذا القرار المشترك وكذا موجز من الاتفاقية المذكورة في الجريدة الرسمية.”

    “رئيس الاتحاد العام لمقاولات المغرب”

    Official gazette, Arabic general edition, pages 5224 to 5229; the French translation edition had not been published on the retrieval date (French editions up to 7544 were checked). Excerpts were transcribed from the page images, because the PDF's text layer reverses Arabic word order; automated excerpt matching cannot be relied on for this record. The orders approve conventions that are annexed to the original orders; the gazette does not reproduce them and, as article 2 of each order provides, publishes only a summary of each. This registry has not obtained the signed texts. The record is a legal establishment act, not the signed text, and it gives no signing dates. Each group is created for thirty years with a statutory seat (JAZARI Industrie X.0 at the Euro-Mediterranean University of Fès). Legal establishment is not operation: the gazette does not show premises in use, staff or activity. The summaries name further founding members (for JAZARI ROOT, the Secretary-General of the Government, several ministries, national police, the CNDP, the Digital Development Agency and the Rabat-Salé-Kénitra regional council; for JAZARI EduTech and JAZARI Smart City, further ministries and public bodies; the Industrie X.0 summary names no members beyond the seven recorded here) that are not recorded as relationships here. This issue (Bulletin Officiel 7534) contains no act concerning the Al Jazari institute in Nador; that does not show that no such act exists elsewhere.

  • MAI-EV-0008VERIFIED FACT

    Signature of the constitutive convention of the public-interest group "Institut Jazari ROOT"

    On 12 January 2026, at the official launch of the "AI Made in Morocco" programme organised by the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform, the president of CGEM signed the constitutive convention of the public-interest group (GIP) "Institut Jazari ROOT" alongside several ministerial departments and public institutions.

    “Casablanca, le 12 janvier 2026 - La Confédération Générale des Entreprises du Maroc (CGEM) a pris part à la cérémonie officielle de lancement du programme “AI Made in Morocco – L’IA au cœur de la transformation numérique et des services publics”, organisée par le Ministère de la Transition Numérique et de la Réforme de l’Administration.”

    “a procédé à la signature de la convention constitutive du Groupement d’Intérêt Public (GIP) “Institut Jazari ROOT”, aux côtés de plusieurs départements ministériels et d’institutions publiques”
