Registry / Data

MAI-DATA — Registry v0.10.0 · as of 20 October 2026

Using the registry data

The registry is published as JSON files that can be read and checked without this site's code. This page describes the files and their identifiers, where each statement comes from, what null and absent values mean, how versions work, and which answers can be recomputed from the data.

Rights. No content or data licence has been declared for the registry. Each record quotes a third-party source, and those quotations remain subject to their publishers' rights: nothing here grants a right to republish source material. TheMoroccoAI is independent and is not affiliated with the Government of Morocco or any institution it lists.

01Files

Start with /data/manifest.json. It lists each file with its role and the SHA-256 of the version published in the same build. Canonical files are maintained by hand and validated on every build; derived files are generated from them and say so in derived_from. Every file is plain UTF-8 JSON and needs no authentication.

02Identifiers

  • Evidence records: MAI-EV-NNNN (evidence_id in evidence.json). Readable at /registry/evidence/#MAI-EV-NNNN.
  • Entities: a lowercase slug (id in ontology.json), also the page address /registry/<id>/.
  • Relationships: have no separate identifier. Each is the triple subject, predicate, object, which the validator keeps unique; matrix.json writes it as subject|predicate|object.
  • Gaps: GAP-NN, readable at /registry/gaps/#gap-nn.
  • Defined values: classifications, verification statuses, entity types, lifecycle stages, project kinds, origins, domains, predicates and gap states are keys of dictionaries that define them in the same file.

Published identifiers are not reused. A record, entity or relationship may be corrected or withdrawn; each such change is listed in the changelog, and a withdrawn identifier is never assigned to anything else. A record whose claim later evidence contradicts is kept and marked superseded_by.

Since v0.1.0, no evidence or entity identifier has been removed or reused: every identifier published in any version is still present, with the same source address for records and the same type for entities. Earlier versions are not published as files; a test checks this against every version of the data in the public repository's history on each build, and anyone can repeat it from that history. One relationship has been replaced as a correction (v0.2.0).

03Provenance

  • Every entity cites records in evidence; a lifecycle stage cites lifecycle_evidence, an origin origin_evidence, and each attribute its own record.
  • Every relationship cites records in evidence and carries one classification, which is never stronger than its records support.
  • Every record gives the source_url, source_organization, source_type, publication_date, retrieval_date, verbatim source_excerpts in source_language, and a verification_status with a verification_note that states the record's limits.
  • claim is the registry's summary of what the source documents; the excerpts are the source's own words.
  • A record's classification describes what its source documents, not whether a plan was delivered or a target met.
  • A signature is documented by a report of it unless a record of the signed text itself is cited (evidence_type signed_instrument). Such a record is admitted only when the registry has read the signed text in full, from a party to it or an official publisher of it; a report, summary, press release or news item about the signing never qualifies, even from a signatory or the government. An official gazette text approving an instrument, with or without a published summary of it, records a legal act about the instrument: it is neither the signed text nor a report of the signing, and it never qualifies either. The validator checks what it can (classification, verification, source type, the instrument's title, date, signatories and declared access, and that each signatory_of relationship citing it names one of its signatories); that the text was in fact read is an editorial requirement. No such record exists yet: every signature in the registry rests on a report of the signing, and some also cite an official gazette record of the instrument's approval.

04Null, absent and unknown values

  • publication_date: null means the source is undated. Use retrieval_date, the day the source was read, which is always present. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD, or YYYY-MM where the source gives only a month.
  • An absent optional field means "not recorded", never "no" or "none". For example, an entity without lifecycle is of a type that has no stage, and a record without status_evidence declares no stage.
  • In matrix.json, object.lifecycle and object.stage are null for entity types without a stage.
  • An empty list means none is recorded here, not that none exists.
  • A defined value (classification, stage, predicate, gap state) that is not defined in its file is an error. A consumer should stop rather than guess its meaning; the example below does.
  • The values accepted for source_type, evidence_type and status_basis are listed in the manifest under vocabularies.
  • Free-text fields (status, note, summary, qualifications) are for readers and are not a controlled vocabulary.

05Versions and updates

  • evidence.json and ontology.json carry the same version and as_of date, which the validator enforces; derived files carry them as registry_version and as_of. as_of is the date the registry's sources were last checked.
  • The registry version changes, with a changelog entry, whenever the published reference data or its meaning changes: records, entities, relationships, gap assessments or the definitions they use. An editorial change that affects neither, such as a typo in a note, may be published without a new version. Every change is a commit in the public repository.
  • data/gaps.json and data/changelog.json carry no version of their own: their place in a build is shown by the manifest only.
  • Fetch the manifest first and compare each file's SHA-256. A mismatch means the file came from a different build, for example while a new version propagates through caches; fetch again later.
  • The hashes are published by the same site as the files. They show that the files you fetched belong to one build and were not changed after it; they are not a signature, and they do not show who published the files or that their content is accurate. Accuracy rests on the cited sources, which each record links to.
  • When the data a gap rests on changes, the build fails until the gap is re-assessed. Each gap in data/gaps.json stores the records it was assessed against and a digest of that data (assessed_fingerprint), which a consumer can recompute.
  • A gap with entity_assessments is assessed per entity: for each entity, the records documenting each evidence dimension defined in entity_assessment (legal approval, governance event, staffing, premises, operational activity, outputs, documented non-commencement, documented discontinuation). Only a VERIFIED FACT about the entity can document a dimension. An entity resolves only through a published resolution path (documented operation, documented non-commencement, or documented discontinuation after operation), never on a legal approval, a governance event or missing records, and stays listed while the others remain open; the gap resolves when every entity has. An empty dimension means no qualifying record is recorded here, not that the thing does not exist.

06Coverage and limits

The registry holds what it has documented so far: 63 records, 65 entities and 78 relationships. It is not a complete map of Morocco's AI ecosystem, and an absent organisation, relationship or record is a gap in coverage. Public availability is not use, a signature or an announcement is not delivery, and a target is not an outcome. The methodology sets out what the registry does not establish, and the gap register lists 7 questions it cannot yet answer. Whether others use the data, and how, is not measured.

07Which answers can be recomputed

An answer is reproducible when it follows from the published data and published rules. Some parts rest on judgments that the data records but cannot derive; they are marked below. matrix.json publishes the rules for its derived fields, row order and caveats under derivation; registry/gaps/gaps.json publishes the gap checks and the digest algorithm.

Reference state

reference-state.json publishes projections of the canonical data, each with its rule under derivation and the SHA-256 of the canonical files it was computed from (derived_from). It is computed from those files only; the manifest, which lists the reference state's own SHA-256, is generated after it. Every reference projection is reproducible from the published canonical data. Fixed contract metadata is explicitly labelled recorded-not-derivable. Where a field copies or counts a recorded editorial judgment (a classification, a lifecycle stage, a gap's state), its rule names that judgment under rests_on. It describes what this registry records, not Morocco's AI ecosystem.

It deliberately does not include:

  • Any score, confidence or completeness measure, and any single evidence state per entity.
  • Any list or count of conflicts between sources: conflicts are recorded in gap statements and record notes, not in a governed field.
  • Any measure, count or statement of progress towards a target, of targets met or not met, or of indicators.
  • Any sum of amounts, including appropriations for different years.
  • Any rank computed by the registry, including a rank on the Online Services Index.
  • Any classification of entities by first-party sources.
  • domain, or any role, function or status of an actor inferred from it.
  • Free-text summaries, notes or statuses, except the notes inside the verbatim copy of entity_assessments; each entity's reasons are in ontology.json.

08Checking the data yourself

/examples/registry_consumer.py (also in the repository) reads only the published JSON. It uses the Python 3.10+ standard library and does not import this site's code. It stops with an error if a reference is missing, a value is undefined or the files do not belong to the same build.

  • python3 registry_consumer.py verify: checks the manifest and every reference. It also recomputes the matrix, the GAP-05 list, each gap's data checks and digest, and every reproducible field of reference-state.json, validates its fixed contract metadata, and compares them with the published files.
  • python3 registry_consumer.py relationship ministry-digital-transition operates idarati-ai: a relationship, its classification, and each supporting record with its source, dates and limits.
  • python3 registry_consumer.py gap GAP-03: what a gap establishes and does not, with the result of its data checks.
  • python3 registry_consumer.py q8 and q9: the reproducible parts of those answers.

By default it reads https://themoroccoai.com. Use --source with another address or a local copy of the site.

09Changes to the data contract

A breaking change makes data that a consumer accepted under one version fail under the next. Each is listed here, in the manifest (breaking_changes) and in the changelog. Old values are not accepted as aliases, so a consumer built for an earlier version fails rather than misreads the data.

0.5.0/registry/matrix/matrix.json rows[].documentation_basisincludes-formal-recordincludes-non-announcement-recordThe value marks a formal-act relationship that cites at least one record other than an announcement: an official report, a regulatory record, a repository, a publication, a ranking or a direct observation. The old name described these as formal records of the act, which for signatures read as the signed text; no such text is recorded for any signature. The rows it applies to are unchanged; only the name changed. In v0.10.0 it applies to 22 rows.A consumer that compares this field with the old value fails: the v0.4.1 example consumer exits with status 1 and reports, for each row, "has documentation_basis 'includes-non-announcement-record'; the published data gives 'includes-formal-record'".Replace the old value with the new one, or read the names from derivation.documentation_basis.assignment in matrix.json, as the v0.5.0 example consumer does.

Other changes in v0.5.0 add to the data without changing what existing values mean: a sentence in the qualifications of every Signed row, a sixth gap (GAP-06), and the evidence type signed_instrument in the manifest's vocabularies. A consumer that compares qualifications or gap lists exactly will see the additions.

10Corrections

Send corrections, with the record or entity identifier and a source, to agent@sohadot.com. Corrections are made in the canonical files and re-validated, then published as a new version, with the change listed in the changelog.
